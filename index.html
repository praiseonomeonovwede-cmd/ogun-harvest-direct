HOW OUR SUPPLY CHAIN WORKS

Direct Outgrower Partnerships

Traditional produce passes through up to four middlemen before reaching city markets, inflating prices and causing heavy spoilage. We work directly with over 400 registered farmers across Abeokuta, Ijebu-Ode, Makurdi, and Jos.

Farmers receive guaranteed off-take agreements and fair instant payouts, while your business enjoys predictable weekly pricing insulated from arbitrary market spikes.

Post-Harvest Grading & Crate Packing

Spoilage happens when delicate produce is crushed inside woven sacks during interstate transit. At our regional aggregation centers, every harvest is washed, sorted by size and ripeness, and packed into stackable ventilated crates.

You pay strictly for usable, kitchen-ready yield—cutting your prep waste by up to 30% every single week.

Reliable Dawn Dispatch

Restaurants and retail shelves cannot wait for late deliveries. Our night-haul fleet moves produce from farm hubs directly to city distribution points, arriving at your loading bay between 5:30 AM and 8:00 AM.

Every delivery comes with digital weight receipts, transparent batch tracking, and flexible weekly invoicing for verified corporate accounts.
